### Added

- Record a meeting on the iPhone: tap record, name it (or leave it blank to name it by time), record, stop, then save or discard. Saved meetings show in a list with their date and length.
- A microphone-permission screen that opens iOS Settings when the microphone was refused.

### Security

- Pinned the uuid library under Expo's config tooling to a patched line.
